What AirThere provides

A reliable visual record of progress, built for clear communication.

AirThere documents projects at agreed intervals from repeatable set locations and multiple site angles. Each report creates an easy-to-share record that helps developers, builders, owners, financiers, consultants and project teams understand current status without relying on fragmented updates.
